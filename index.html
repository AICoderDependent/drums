Hear the video through the kit
The video plays on the tablet, so its sound leaves the tablet. Two ways to send it to the kit (the A50 listing shows both):
1. USB sound. With the kit connected by USB, swipe down on the tablet, tap the volume or the speaker icon, and look for "Media output" or "Output device". Choose the kit (USB audio). Turn up the kit's USB or audio volume.
2. Cable. Use a 3.5 mm stereo cable from the tablet's headphone socket (or a USB-C adapter) to the kit's Audio Input. Turn up the kit's audio input volume.
The app cannot choose this for you. Android and Chrome decide where the sound goes. Sound that arrives through USB can be a little late, so use Video sync in the video settings.

How to use it

1. Add and open a video. 2. Press play on the video. 3. Press Tap tempo in time with the music (8 taps or more), or type the BPM. 4. At the first beat of a bar press "Bar 1 starts here", then use the 10 ms buttons and the Click to line it up. 5. Pick a groove. Choose another groove at the start of the chorus. 6. Play along. Use the speed slider to slow the video down.

The app cannot hear the video, so live recordings that speed up or slow down will drift. Studio recordings work best. Some videos cannot be played inside other apps. Ads may play before a video.

Exercises and songs

How to read the notes

Stars: 1 star = 80 % or better, 2 stars = 90 % or better, 3 stars = 90 % or better at the goal tempo. Stars count in Play along mode when you play the whole exercise from the start.

Check my kit

1. What does the kit send?

Hit pads on the kit. Each line below is one message. If nothing appears, the tablet is not receiving the kit.

Waiting for the kit...

2. Does the kit make the sound?

Tap a button. You should hear that drum from the kit (not from the tablet).

3. Teach the app your kit

Hit that pad or zone once on the kit.

4. Choose a kit sound from the app (experimental)

The app plays the song on the kit, so it always uses the sound you chose on the drum module. The app cannot see which sound that is. This button asks the module to change kit. It only works if the A50 accepts the request, so try it.

5. Still a problem?

Press Copy report and send it to your helper. It lists the ports and the last messages.

Practice

With strict off, any hit on the ride or hi-hat counts. With strict on, the ride inner part and the hi-hat pedal notes need the right zone.

Lanes: tap a lane name under Lanes to change it. A filled dot means play it. A half dot means guide: you see it faintly and hear it, but you do not need to play it. An empty dot means off: hidden and silent.

On a computer: keys 1 to 8 play the lanes, B is the ride inner part, F is the hi-hat pedal, and Space starts and pauses.
